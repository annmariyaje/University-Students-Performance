# 🎓 University Students Performance Analysis 2026

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes the academic performance, study habits, lifestyle factors, and technology usage of university students.

The dataset contains **10,000 university students from 13 countries and 10 academic majors**. The analysis was performed using Power BI to identify patterns and relationships between student habits and academic performance.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze university students' academic performance
- Study the relationship between study hours and GPA
- Analyze the impact of sleep, screen time, social media, and stress
- Explore AI tool usage among students
- Compare academic performance across majors and countries
- Analyze gender-based academic performance
- Identify lifestyle factors associated with student performance

## 📁 Dataset

**Dataset:** University Students Performance & Study Habits 2026

- Total Students: 10,000
- Countries: 13
- Academic Majors: 10
- Average GPA: 3.62 / 4.0

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 📚 Study Hours
GPA increases from **2.89 for students studying 0 hours/day to 4.0 for students studying 8+ hours/day**.

### 😴 Sleep
Average GPA increases from **3.41 at 4 hours of sleep to 3.81 at 10 hours of sleep**.

### 🧠 Stress
Students with low stress have an average GPA of **3.74**, compared with **3.36** for students with high stress.

### 📱 Screen Time
Performance score decreases as daily screen time increases, from **95.3 at 2 hours to 90.8 at 11 hours**.

### 📱 Social Media
Average GPA decreases from **3.76 with 0 hours of social media to 3.37 at 6 hours**.

### 🤖 AI Tool Usage
AI tools are distributed relatively evenly among students:
- ChatGPT – 21.2%
- Gemini – 20.9%
- Claude – 20.5%
- Copilot – 20.1%
- None – 17.2%

### 🌍 Countries & Majors
GPA differences between majors and countries are relatively small, with average GPA values concentrated around **3.61–3.64**.
